No new theory, just a challenge that uses this part's HTML: labels, names, input types, length limits, and a question over a group.

Every passenger leaves someone behind on Earth: a parent, a friend. If something goes wrong on a flight, the station needs to call them. And soon, styles and a script will work on this form, so they need a way to find it.

---

## Mission: Someone to call

Your booking form asks about the passenger, the flight, the seat and the extras, but not about anyone back home.

**Example**: before the notes, a new frame with a question such as "Someone we can call on Earth", and in it a box for their name and one for their phone number, each with its label. Fill in the whole form and send: the console's line has `contact-name=Lena Park&contact-phone=+44 20 7946 0000` in it.

- The form has the id `booking`. Later lessons use it.
- A group with a question over it holds the contact's name (id and name `contact-name`) and phone number (id and name `contact-phone`), each with a label that takes you to its box.
- On a phone, the contact's phone box shows the keyboard for calling, and their name takes at most 40 characters.
- Both are optional: a booking without them is sent, and a whole booking, every box filled in, sends them all.

Psst, the id goes on the `<form>` itself :)
