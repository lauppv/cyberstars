You've built a whole home page, one piece at a time, with a lesson beside you for each. Now build a page on your own, from nothing: a profile for one member of the crew. It's your project, outside the station site, so nothing here touches the pages you've built.

There's no example to follow this time. Everything you need is in the lessons before this one; open them again whenever you're unsure.

## Your files

The folder starts empty. Make three files with **New file**:

- `index.html`, the page;
- `style.css`, its style sheet;
- `script.js`, its script.

## What the page holds

Pick a crew member, real or made up: a name, a job on board, a few things they're good at.

**The page**

- in English, able to show any letter, with **Crew Profile** on its tab;
- the crew member's name as its one biggest heading;
- a paragraph in the class `role` with their job on board;
- at least two sections with headings of their own, about whatever you like: their skills, their last mission, their favorite spot on the station;
- something marked as important somewhere in the text;
- a link that writes them an email.

**The look**, in `style.css`, linked from the page

- the name centered;
- the job in italics;
- colors, a font and sizes of your choice, readable, the text sizes in `rem`;
- nothing to scroll sideways on a phone.

**The script**, in `script.js`, deferred

- a button with the id `salute` and an empty paragraph with the id `greeting`;
- a click on the button writes in the greeting the name from the biggest heading, followed by ` salutes you!`. For **Ada Okafor**, it reads `Ada Okafor salutes you!`.

Run the tests whenever you like: they say what's still missing.
