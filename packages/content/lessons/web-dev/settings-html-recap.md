No new theory, just a challenge that uses this part's HTML: real buttons rather than ARIA, buttons that say they're pressed, and icons only for the eye.

Some visitors feel dizzy when things move on a screen. The settings page should let them ask for less motion. The animations come later in this part: today, the buttons.

---

## Mission: Less motion

**Example**: under the theme part, a part with a heading such as "Motion", a sentence about it, and two buttons, `✦ Full` and `◌ Less`. Full is pressed, and looks it, like the System button.

- After the theme part, a new part with a heading of its own and a box with the id `motion-buttons`, holding two real buttons that send no form: `motion-full`, which says `Full`, and `motion-less`, which says `Less`.
- Full says it's pressed, and Less says it isn't, the way a screen reader understands.
- Each button has an icon before its word, which a screen reader skips. The icons are yours.
- The pressed button looks different from the other, and both are readable, with a contrast of at least 4.5.

Psst, the pressed look of the theme buttons can count for these buttons too :)
