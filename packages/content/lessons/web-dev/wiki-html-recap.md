```text
Tools & Spare Parts
- a broken pump can't wait for the next supply ship, so the station keeps its own tools and spare parts on board
- where they are: the cargo bay, on the lower deck; Tomas Okafor, the engineer, keeps a list of every part
- nothing falls on the station, it floats: every tool is tied to a belt, and every box of parts closes with a lid
- facts: 1,200 spare parts; the heaviest tool weighs 12 kg; the longest cable is 30 m
```
